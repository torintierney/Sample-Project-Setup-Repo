# Risk-Based Requirement Gaps

## Purpose
Identify requirements that are missing or under-specified in the current PRD based on the risk register, mitigation matrix, and discovery inputs.

Goal: convert key risk controls into explicit requirements instead of leaving them as implied expectations or open validation items.

## Criticality Guidance

- Must-have: Required for release readiness, compliance posture, operational viability, or decision quality.
- Should-have: Important for adoption, maintainability, or efficiency but can be phased if explicitly acknowledged.

---

## Common Missing-Control Requirement Areas

Use this checklist to seed the table:

- Metadata source-of-truth and mismatch handling
- Lifecycle state tracking and queue visibility
- Operational dashboards and review metrics
- Quantified performance and capacity targets
- Benchmark and validation gate requirements
- Integration posture and contract-test requirements
- Audit, retention, and privileged-action logging
- Segregation-of-duties and approval boundaries
- Governed config promotion, rollback, and audit history
- Training, user readiness, and support requirements
- Explicit phase non-goals and scope guardrails
- Named ownership for data, controls, and release evidence
- Measurable pilot or release exit criteria
