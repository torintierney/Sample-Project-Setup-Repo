# Risk Communication Strategy

## Purpose
Tailor project risk communication for each audience so decisions happen at the right level, with the right amount of detail, at the right time.

Use this artifact alongside:
- Risk Register
- Risk Mitigation Matrix
- PRD or delivery plan

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Purpose
Provide a fast view of the few risks that can materially change scope, timeline, business case, or stakeholder confidence.

### Key Messages
- [Message 1: biggest near-term risk framing]
- [Message 2: highest business-impact risk areas]
- [Message 3: decisions required now]
- [Message 4: sponsor role in trade-off decisions]

### Top 3-5 Risks To Surface

1. **[Risk title]**  
Business impact: [What happens to value, timeline, cost, or confidence]  
Decision framing: [What decision is needed now and trade-off options]

2. **[Risk title]**  
Business impact: [Impact]  
Decision framing: [Decision needed]

3. **[Risk title]**  
Business impact: [Impact]  
Decision framing: [Decision needed]

4. **[Optional risk title]**  
Business impact: [Impact]  
Decision framing: [Decision needed]

5. **[Optional risk title]**  
Business impact: [Impact]  
Decision framing: [Decision needed]

### Level of Detail
- Keep to 3-5 risks only.
- Focus on business impact, trade-offs, and decisions.
- Avoid implementation details unless they affect scope, funding, or approval timing.

### Format Recommendation
- Primary format: one-page briefing or one summary slide.
- Backup format: short talking points for sponsor check-ins.

### Suggested Frequency
- [e.g., weekly during pre-project decision closure]
- [e.g., biweekly during pilot]
- Immediate ad hoc escalation when red-risk triggers occur.

## Project Team

### Purpose
Give the delivery team the full working risk picture so they can manage execution, monitor signals, and apply mitigations before escalation is required.

### Key Messages
- [Message 1: use full register, not just top risks]
- [Message 2: risks must have owners/signals/actions]
- [Message 3: early validation disciplines]
- [Message 4: embed risk work in sprint and release flow]

### What The Team Should See
- Full risk register with likelihood, impact, rationale.
- Mitigation matrix with owners, preventive controls, detection signals, and response actions.
- Monitoring cadence and escalation triggers.
- Requirement ambiguities and unresolved dependencies tied to risk items.

### Level of Detail
- Full detail is appropriate.
- Include thresholds, fallback paths, and dependency owners.
- Distinguish what can be managed locally vs what needs steering/sponsor intervention.

### Format Recommendation
- Primary format: detailed working document plus recurring workshop review.
- Operational pattern: review register in weekly workstream meetings; apply mitigation matrix in sprint planning and release-readiness checks.

### Suggested Frequency
- [e.g., weekly during Phase 0 and Phase 1]
- [e.g., every release candidate review]
- Immediate after major failures, regressions, or dependency shifts.

## Steering Committee

### Purpose
Provide visibility into risks requiring policy decisions, resource commitments, or cross-functional coordination beyond the project team’s authority.

### Key Messages
- [Message 1: steering should focus on decision-grade risks]
- [Message 2: scope, ownership, compliance, and capacity are key]
- [Message 3: escalate before milestones are missed]

### Risks Best Suited For Steering Review
- [Scope expansion risk requiring governance decision]
- [Cross-functional ownership dispute]
- [Compliance/security approval blocker]
- [External dependency delay requiring executive coordination]
- [Resource constraint requiring reallocation]

### Escalation Criteria
- A required decision remains unresolved across planned checkpoints.
- Mitigation requires additional budget, staffing, or policy direction.
- External dependency threatens a milestone or release gate.
- Red risk affects go-live approval, major scope, or business-case assumptions.

### Level of Detail
- Medium detail.
- Include decision owner, deadline, cross-functional impact, and recommendation.
- Exclude day-to-day mitigation mechanics.

### Format Recommendation
- Primary format: steering dashboard or review pack.
- Include RAG risk summary and decision log for escalated items.

### Suggested Frequency
- At each formal steering committee meeting.
- Ad hoc when escalation criteria are met.

## Recommended Message Discipline

- Keep sponsor and steering communications decision-oriented, not diagnostic.
- Keep team communications action-oriented, not abstract.
- Use consistent risk IDs across all audience views.
- Escalate based on decision need, not severity label alone.
