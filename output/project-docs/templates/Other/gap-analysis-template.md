# Gap Analysis

## Purpose
Identify areas where requirements, decisions, or designs have not been adequately addressed. Use this template during discovery or delivery to surface gaps before they become risks or blockers.

## Instructions
- One row per gap area.
- Be specific about what is missing — avoid vague language like "needs more detail."
- Assign a suggested owner even if not confirmed; it signals accountability.
- Prioritize the follow-up actions by risk level.

## Common Gap Areas to Consider
Use these as prompts — add, remove, or rename rows to fit your project:

- Authentication and authorization
- Data privacy and security
- Performance and scalability
- Error handling and escalation workflows
- Metadata source of truth
- Content management and updates
- Monitoring and analytics
- Training and change management
- Compliance and audit requirements
- Integration requirements
- Acceptance criteria and production readiness
- Test and validation strategy
